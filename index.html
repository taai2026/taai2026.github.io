TAAI 2026 Agentic AI and Physical AI for Cross-Domain Sustainable Innovation

The 31st International Conference on Technologies and Applications of Artificial Intelligence (TAAI 2026), hosted by Taiwanese Association for AI (TAAI) and National Taipei University (NTPU), will be held on November 20–21, 2026, at The Great Roots Forestry Spa Resort, New Taipei City, Taiwan.

Paper submission (International & Domestic Tracks) has been finally extended to September 28, 2026, 23:59 AoE (Taipei time: September 29, 2026, 19:59).

Early-bird registration closes October 31, 2026, 23:59 AoE. Acceptance notifications have been sent; accepted authors must submit camera-ready papers and complete presenting-author registration by the same deadline.

Regular registration closes November 13, 2026, 23:59 AoE.

The program and conference handbook are out. See you at TAAI 2026, November 20–21, 2026.

Thank you for joining TAAI 2026! The program, keynote abstracts, and conference handbook remain available on this site.

1 / Highlights

Why TAAI 2026 matters

◦ 1

Cutting-edge themes

Laser-focused on the two defining currents of the moment: agentic AI that reasons and acts, and physical AI that learns through its body in the world.

◦ 2

Cross-disciplinary dialogue

Sustainable innovation across fields (green finance, tech ethics, and industrial applications) in the same room, speaking to one another.

◦ 3

AI × nature

The Great Roots Forestry Spa Resort provides a focused setting for academic exchange, poster discussions, and cross-domain collaboration.

2 / Venue

Venue: The Great Roots Forestry Spa Resort, Sanxia

TAAI 2026 will be held at The Great Roots Forestry Spa Resort in Sanxia District, New Taipei City. The venue offers conference rooms, accommodation, dining, and access to nearby forest facilities.

Explore the venue →
The Great Roots

Forestry Spa Resort, Sanxia

Tucked into the mountains of New Taipei, surrounded by protected forest. Mineral hot-spring pools, canopy walks, and ceremonial Taiwanese hospitality: a fitting backdrop for ideas about nature, intelligence, and what comes next.

3 / Theme

Cross-disciplinary sustainable innovation with agentic and physical AI

What sparks will fly when agentic AI meets physical, embodied intelligence, and both are asked to serve a world that has to last?

TAAI 2026 is jointly hosted by the Taiwanese Association for Artificial Intelligence (TAAI) and National Taipei University (NTPU). This year we're inviting researchers, practitioners, and policy minds to step off the usual circuit and into a forest, to look hard at what cross-domain, sustainable AI actually looks like when you build it, deploy it, and live with its consequences.
