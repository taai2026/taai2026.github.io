TAAI 2026/Call for Papers/High School Special Session

High School Student Session on
Artificial Intelligence and Its Applications

This special session “High School Student Session on Artificial Intelligence and Its Applications” is hosted under TAAI 2026 (Technologies and Applications of Artificial Intelligence).

Artificial intelligence has shown remarkable successes in various real-world applications. The applications of computational intelligence are now shaping the way in various domains, such as businesses, governments, and educational institutions. This special session provides a platform for high school students to increase their participation in the field of computational intelligence. High school students are encouraged to submit the report or results on their special projects in school or self-study projects on any aspect of computational intelligence or its applications.

1 / List of topics

What you can submit

High school students are encouraged to submit reports related to Artificial Intelligence or its applications, on the following topics, but not limited to:

Artificial Intelligence
Machine Learning
Data Mining
Applications of Artificial Intelligence
Intelligent learning systems
Intelligent monitoring and tutoring systems
Smart classroom
Recommendation Systems
Classification
Clustering
Anomaly Detection
Data analytics and big data applications
2 / Paper submission

Format & how to submit

Submission by email

All papers must be submitted to the following email:
jwhuang@mail.ncku.edu.tw

  • Each paper should be within 4 pages, including figures, tables, and references.
  • Papers must be written in English.
  • Submissions are single-blind, i.e., the names and affiliations of the authors should be listed in the submitted version.
  • All papers should be submitted using the Springer A4 format.

Springer manuscript templates

  • Word template:
    Download (springernature.com)
  • LaTeX template:
    Download (springernature.com)

Registration & presentation

  • Paper registration is required for the inclusion of papers in the conference.
  • One of the authors of the accepted paper must present the work at this special session of TAAI 2026.
  • After the paper is accepted, an authorization agreement needs to be signed.
