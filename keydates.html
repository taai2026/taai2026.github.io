TAAI 2026 Important Dates

From proposal submissions to the conference itself, here is the full TAAI 2026 timeline. Unless noted otherwise, all deadlines fall at 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth).

Call for papers → Registration
1 / Important dates

All deadlines are 23:59 AoE.

2 / Good to know

A few notes on the schedule.

Dates are subject to change. Any adjustments will be announced on this page and through the official conference channels.

We recommend submitting ahead of the deadline — last-day system load can be heavy, and AoE means the cut-off is later than most local clocks suggest.
