TAAI 2026/Sponsorship

TAAI 2026 Sponsorship Program

TAAI 2026, the 31st International Conference on Technologies and Applications of Artificial Intelligence, gathers 300+ researchers, graduate students, and industry technologists for two days on Agentic AI and Physical AI. Put your brand at the center of the conversation.

November 20–21, 2026 · The Great Roots Forestry Spa Resort, New Taipei City · Organized by TAAI & NTPU

View packages Contact the team
1 / Why sponsor

Five reasons to partner with TAAI 2026

Held annually since 1995, TAAI is Taiwan's longest-running AI conference, with an international track published in Springer CCIS and a bilateral exchange with the Japanese Society for Artificial Intelligence (JSAI).

◦ 1

Brand visibility

Reach hundreds of AI professors, researchers, and graduate students at Taiwan's flagship academic AI event.

◦ 2

Talent recruitment

Meet master's and PhD candidates in AI from universities across Taiwan, before anyone else does.

◦ 3

Industry–academia links

Open joint-research and technology-transfer conversations through industry talks, booths, and matchmaking.

◦ 4

International exposure

An international track and JSAI exchange carry your brand to the global research community.

◦ 5

Sustainability story

Align with the theme of cross-domain sustainable innovation and back the next generation of AI talent.

4 / How to sponsor

Four steps, one email

◦ 1

Reach out

Email taai2026org@gmail.com with your intended tier (recommended by September 30, 2026).

◦ 2

Sign & pay

The TAAI secretariat sends the sponsorship agreement and payment details (bank transfer or postal remittance).

◦ 3

Send materials

Submit your logo, artwork, and booth requirements by October 23, 2026 for program production.

◦ 4

Join us

Register complimentary attendees by November 6, then enjoy the conference on November 20–21.

5 / Terms

Terms and conditions

  1. All amounts are in NTD, exclusive of tax. Official receipts are issued by the Taiwanese Association for Artificial Intelligence (TAAI).
  2. Complimentary registrations must be completed by November 6, 2026; unused slots after the deadline are forfeited.
  3. Logo size and placement follow sponsorship tier; within a tier, the order in which payment is received.
  4. Artwork, logo files, and booth requirements are due by October 23, 2026. Benefits without materials by the deadline are deemed waived and are non-refundable.
  5. Attendee lists are not provided. Sponsors may collect information at their booths with attendees' consent.
  6. If the event moves online due to force majeure, the fee is adjusted to 60% of the original amount, with the difference refunded or credited to the following year.
  7. The organizer reserves the right to make final adjustments and decisions regarding the packages and benefits listed here.
