3 / Submission

Guidelines & format

Submission tracks

TAAI 2026 offers three submission tracks, all managed under a single OpenReview venue:

  • International Track
  • Domestic Track
  • High School Session

What to know before you submit

  • Papers must follow the Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) format.
  • Submissions are handled through OpenReview.
  • Paper submission deadline: September 14, 2026.
  • Double-blind review — anonymize your manuscript.
  • Accepted papers require at least one author to register and present on-site.
  • Best Paper, Best Student Paper, and Best Industry Paper awards will be announced at the banquet.

OpenReview profile moderation

If you do not yet have an OpenReview profile, please create one well ahead of the deadline:

  • New profiles created without an institutional email go through a moderation process that can take up to two weeks.
  • New profiles created with an institutional email are activated automatically.
